# JobForge Helper, browser extension

A Chrome extension (Manifest V3) that connects your daily browsing to your local JobForge cockpit. It turns the LinkedIn and Naukri subscriptions you already pay for into captured, scored, scam screened pipeline entries, and it prefills application forms from your approved answers.

## What it does

* **Capture.** On any job posting you open, a floating Send to JobForge button appears. One click sends the posting's title, company, location, description, and links to your local cockpit, where it is classified, scored, and scam screened within a second. LinkedIn, Naukri, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Workday, and any page embedding schema.org JobPosting structured data are supported. Postings are captured once; repeats are detected and skipped.
* **Autofill with memory.** On application forms, an Autofill with JobForge button fills recognized fields from your saved data: name, email, phone, city, LinkedIn, GitHub, notice period, experience answer, relocation stance, expected pay only where you saved one, and a drafted motivation text for long answer boxes. Every value it fills is remembered, and every value is editable in the options panel, so a wrong fill is corrected once and never repeats. Corrections saved in the app under Settings, Autofill data, flow in through the Sync button, and local edits stick until the next sync.
* **Pairing.** The extension talks only to your JobForge server on localhost, guarded by a token you paste from the Settings page. No other server, no telemetry, no remote code.

The focused, step by step manual for installing the extension and running quick Naukri and LinkedIn application loops lives in [docs/EXTENSION_GUIDE.md](../docs/EXTENSION_GUIDE.md).

## What it deliberately does not do

* It never presses a submit button. The final click on every application is yours, on every platform.
* It never reads pages you have not opened, never runs in the background, and never harvests listings in bulk. It is a per posting capture tool driven by your clicks, not a scraper.
* It never automates activity on LinkedIn or Naukri beyond what you click, because both platforms prohibit automated account activity in their terms and the realistic penalty is your own account.

## Install (Chrome or Edge, two minutes)

1. Open chrome://extensions and enable Developer mode.
2. Click Load unpacked and select this `extension` folder.
3. Pin the JobForge Helper icon, open it, paste the token from JobForge Settings, and press Pair, then Test connection.

## Regenerating icons

The icons are plain PNG files generated by `build-icons.mjs`. If you ever want to restyle them, run:

```bash
node extension/build-icons.mjs
```

## How the capture works, briefly

The content script reads schema.org JobPosting structured data from the page, which LinkedIn, Naukri, and all major ATS boards embed for search engines, with platform specific fallbacks. Nothing is sent anywhere except your local JobForge server, and only when you press the button.
